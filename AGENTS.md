## Editorial content workflow

Before changing blog, FAQ, product-page copy, metadata, or internal links, read:

1. `content-system/PROJECT_INSTRUCTIONS.md`
2. `content-system/site-profile.yaml`
3. `content-system/editorial-rules.md`

Drafts and research live in `content-system/`. The Payload CMS remains the publishing source of truth. A commit or pull request does not authorize CMS publication.
