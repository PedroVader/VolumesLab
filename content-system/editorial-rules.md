# Editorial rules

## Purpose

The blog is an extension of product FAQs. It explains tests, measurement methods, reports, and practical differences between methods. It is not a health-advice publication.

## Public-copy rules

- Write in clear British English for readers in Madrid.
- Be direct, restrained, factual, and specific.
- Use no em dashes.
- Do not use these terms in public copy: `longevity`, `biohacking`, `biological age`.
- Avoid hype, fear, universal claims, thought leadership, and content that sounds generated from a generic template.
- Do not say a method is the "gold standard" when a precise description is available.
- Do not imply that a device, scan, or single result diagnoses health status.

## Prohibited content

- Medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment guidance.
- Exercise, nutrition, supplement, peptide, or medication prescriptions.
- Claims that a test predicts lifespan or prevents a condition.
- Unsupported claims of superiority, accuracy, precision, safety, or outcomes.
- Invented first-hand experience, customer stories, quotes, reviews, data, or staff sign-off.
- A claim that Volumes Lab offers a test or protocol unless it is confirmed in the catalogue or by the client.

## Review gate

Every article requires client review word by word. Content that describes clinical interpretation, eligibility, contraindications, or patient-specific meaning also requires review by the appropriate qualified professional before CMS entry.

## Product-page strategy

Use cases belong inside the relevant product page under a `Who is it for?` section. Do not create a separate landing page for each sport or audience unless later search and conversion evidence shows meaningful incremental value.
