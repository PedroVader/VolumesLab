# Research-led content operating manual

The editorial system exists to investigate demand, understand the destination website, create or improve useful content, implement it safely, and verify observable outcomes. It does not exist to produce the maximum number of pages or words.

## Modes

- `STUDY`: establish facts without content changes.
- `AUDIT`: identify gaps and priorities.
- `CREATE`: research, brief, and draft a new page after checking cannibalization.
- `REFRESH`: improve an existing page while preserving valid intent.
- `QA`: check editorial, evidence, links, metadata, implementation, and presentation.
- `PUBLISH`: publish only the explicitly authorized scope.

## Evidence contract

Keep these categories separate:

- `VERIFIED FACT`: supported by a cited primary or authoritative source.
- `EXTERNAL OBSERVATION`: an identifiable third party's report.
- `FIRST-HAND`: supported by documented project evidence.
- `INFERENCE`: an editorial conclusion derived from disclosed facts.
- `UNKNOWN`: not publishable as certainty.

For health-related subjects, use conservative wording, primary sources, visible limitations, and human review. Never invent results, testimonials, experience, credentials, specifications, availability, or medical conclusions.

## Brief and draft gate

Every proposed article needs one dated research ledger and one brief. The brief must define the direct answer, audience, exclusions, available evidence, prohibited claims, internal links, technical implications, and acceptance criteria. Draft only when evidence is sufficient for the planned claims.

## Quality gates

1. Scope and cannibalization
2. Editorial clarity and brand fit
3. Evidence and claim traceability
4. Originality
5. Internal and external links
6. Media provenance and accessibility
7. Metadata, canonical, structured data, and sitemap behavior
8. Build and validation
9. Desktop and mobile presentation
10. Explicit publication authorization and live verification

Publication remains a separate action even when the draft, code, and review are complete.
