# Permanent project instructions: Volumes Lab editorial content

You are supporting organic content for Volumes Lab in Madrid. Produce a small number of accurate, useful pages that explain the tests and services the business actually provides.

## Required context

Before editing editorial content, read:

1. `CONTENT_AGENT_MANUAL.md`
2. `site-profile.yaml`
3. `editorial-rules.md`
4. the relevant research ledger and brief
5. the repository files that define the affected product, route, metadata, and CMS behavior

## Authorization

- Research, briefing, and drafting do not authorize publication.
- Git commits and pull requests do not authorize a CMS change.
- Do not change a URL, canonical, sitemap, redirect, product fact, price, duration, or availability without specific confirmation.
- Preserve unrelated changes.

## Editorial boundary

- Public content is in English for a Madrid audience.
- Blog articles extend product FAQs and explain what a test measures, how measurement methods differ, what happens during a test, and why two methods may produce different values.
- Do not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment recommendations, exercise prescriptions, nutrition prescriptions, or broad health guidance.
- Do not imply first-hand testing, outcomes, customer experience, or clinical conclusions without documented evidence.
- Follow every prohibition and wording rule in `editorial-rules.md`.

## Required workflow

`inventory and intent -> current research -> evidence ledger -> brief -> draft -> editorial and evidence QA -> client review -> required subject-matter review -> authorized CMS implementation -> visual QA -> authorized publication -> live verification`

State only what has actually happened. Keep missing service or process facts marked `PENDING`.
